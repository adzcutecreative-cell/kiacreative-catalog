## BAGIAN 3 — FAQ

### Judul
```
Frequently asked questions
```

### Isi
```
Q: How do I get my files?
A: All products are instant digital downloads. After payment, Etsy or Gumroad gives you a ZIP file. No physical item is shipped.

Q: Do I need special software?
A: Most spreadsheets work in Microsoft Excel and free Google Sheets. Web apps open in any modern browser — no hosting, API key or subscription needed. Canva templates work with a free Canva account.

Q: Can I edit the templates?
A: Yes. Colors, text, categories and layouts are editable in Google Sheets, Excel or Canva.

Q: Can I use these for my business?
A: Yes for personal and client work. Commercial resale of the source files is not included unless the listing says "resell rights" or "PLR".

Q: What if I have a problem with my order?
A: Message the shop on Etsy (or Gumroad) — I usually reply within 24 hours and will help fix the file.

Q: Are these created with AI?
A: Some products were built with generative AI tools for code and design assistance. Every file is reviewed and edited by hand before listing. Listings disclose this where required.

Q: Where do I buy?
A: Etsy → kiacreative.etsy.com (most products)
Gumroad → adzcute.gumroad.com (Dual-AI skills vault, VAT tracker)
```

- Publish ulang setelah tambah teks.
